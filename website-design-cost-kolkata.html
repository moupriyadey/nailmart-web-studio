How much does website making cost in India?

There is no single price for every website. A professional profile website is different from an online store, booking platform or custom client portal. When comparing website design prices in Kolkata or elsewhere, compare the written scope—not only the headline price.

Use this quotation checklist

  • Pages: how many are included, and who supplies the content?
  • Design: mobile-friendly layout, branding and graphics.
  • Contact: phone, WhatsApp, email, map or enquiry form.
  • Technical setup: domain, hosting, SSL, deployment and handover.
  • Search setup: page titles, descriptions, crawlability and sitemap.
  • After delivery: revision rounds, maintenance and future changes.

What affects website design prices in Kolkata?

Pages and content

A short profile website generally takes less work than a large catalogue or multilingual site. Having accurate text, images and contact details ready helps the process.

Features and integrations

WhatsApp links are simpler than custom booking workflows, user accounts, payments or dashboards. Ask for separate pricing for features beyond the starter scope.

Domain, hosting and renewals

Confirm who owns the domain, who pays renewals, where the site is hosted and what happens if you change providers. Distinguish ongoing expenses from a one-time development fee.

Maintenance and future changes

Ask whether support covers technical faults, content edits, backups, security updates or new features—and for how long. Maintenance is included only if the written agreement says so.

How to choose a website designer in Kolkata

  1. Request a written quotation with page limits and deliverables.
  2. Ask to see a live website or a clearly labelled demonstration.
  3. Confirm domain ownership, hosting responsibilities and handover.
  4. Understand payment milestones, delivery assumptions and revisions.
  5. Check that extra costs and recurring charges are disclosed.
  6. Be cautious of promises of guaranteed first-page Google rankings or guaranteed customers.
