GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

We keep the process straightforward and explain additional costs before work begins.

Does ₹4,500 include a domain and hosting?

No. Domain registration, renewal and paid hosting are separate where required. We will explain the options and costs before you commit.

Will a website guarantee more clients or Google rankings?

No. A website can make your information easier to find and contact, but enquiries, revenue and search rankings cannot be guaranteed.

Can you build a dashboard, booking system or client portal?

These features are outside the starter package. We can review the requirements and provide a separate quotation if feasible.

What about advocate website content?

The advocate must approve the content, and it must comply with applicable professional conduct rules. We do not promise or promote legal outcomes.

When does development begin?

After the written scope is approved, the agreement is accepted, the advance is received and the required content is supplied. A typical starter project is estimated at 7–10 working days from that point, subject to client response and scope.
